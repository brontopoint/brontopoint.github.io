One loop, applied to timing and to trust

We use the same four steps for timing problems and for security problems.

  1. Timing and determinism

    Security and trust

  2. MeasureCollect the evidence

    Timing: Profile latency, jitter and interference between workloads on shared hardware.

    Trust: Integrity measurement: measured boot and recorded hashes of what actually loaded and ran.

  3. AttributeExplain the cause

    Timing: Trace each outlier to its source: scheduler, cache, memory bandwidth, interrupt or driver.

    Trust: Tie every measurement to an identity and a chain of trust, so you know who and what it came from.

  4. ControlAct on the cause

    Timing: Configure, isolate or limit: partitioning, scheduling and quality-of-service on the resources that matter.

    Trust: Enforce policy at the boundary: TEEs, hypervisor isolation, key protection and access control.

  5. VerifyProve the result

    Timing: Show the bound holds under load, with repeatable benchmarks and regression evidence.

    Trust: Remote attestation and tamper-evident records that a third party can check.
